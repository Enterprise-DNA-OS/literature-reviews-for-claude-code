## Selection

Nested Knowledge scores 4 annual bill, 5 data-shaped, 1 buyer evidence, 2 search demand, 4 one-session buildable, 5 import path: 21/30. The [vendor pricing page](https://about.nested-knowledge.com/pricing/) lists Business Essentials at $295 per user monthly and Enterprise at $695. Ten Enterprise users model to $83,400 annually at the monthly rate before tax. This is a list-price scenario, not a verified NZ or AU customer invoice. Annual checkout terms can differ. The first Nest and academic pricing have separate terms.

No local customer or CRM snapshot match for Nested Knowledge, DistillerSR or Covidence was found. Buyer score is deliberately one. No live customer-system reads were made. Search results include alternative directories; no keyword volume or verified customer complaint was obtained. The score is a qualitative ranking, not measured demand.

Other candidates: DistillerSR scores 1/5/1/2/4/4 = 17, with the invoice unverified and [pricing quoted privately](https://www.distillersr.com/pricing/corporate-and-government-pricing). Rightsline scores 1/5/1/2/3/3 = 15; its [FAQ quotes privately](https://www.rightsline.com/resources/faq/), and no local invoice establishes the annual-bill threshold. Neither is queued. [Covidence](https://www.covidence.org/pricing/) public packages and [Ziflow Pro](https://www.ziflow.com/pricing) are below the annual-bill threshold; custom tiers have no verified invoice here.
